# Third-party art attribution

## Parnassus, by Anton Raphael Mengs (1761)

`public/art/parnassus.jpg` is a photograph of **Parnassus**, the ceiling
fresco painted in 1761 by **Anton Raphael Mengs** (1728–1779) for the Villa
Albani in Rome. It shows Apollo with the nine Muses, the patrons of music and
the arts, and hangs above the organ on the stage wall of the virtual hall.

- Source file: [Anton Raphael Mengs - Parnassus (FondazioneTorlonia).jpg](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anton_Raphael_Mengs_-_Parnassus_(FondazioneTorlonia).jpg>), Wikimedia Commons
- Credit: Fondazione Torlonia
- Licence: public domain. The painter died more than 100 years ago, and a
  faithful photographic reproduction of a two-dimensional public-domain work
  is itself public domain (PD-Art).

The image is used as published, 2000 × 1115 px. The app trims the scan's thin
black border when it maps the image onto the canvas.
